Our story

Hi, we're Tang Software

Tang Software LLC is an independent studio founded by a small team who got tired of games that treat players like wallets. We wanted to make the kind of games we'd actually want on our own phones — gentle, clever, and a little bit weird.

We build everything ourselves: the art, the code, the music, the words. That's why we ship slowly and only when something feels finished. If you've played one of our games, thank you — it genuinely keeps the lights on.
