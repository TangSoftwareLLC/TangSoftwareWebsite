The premise

What happened in 520?

A letter arrives with no return address — just a key and a number. 520.

The apartment belonged to someone you used to know. Now it's yours, supposedly. But the locks have been changed, the neighbors go quiet when you ask, and the place is exactly as it was the day everything went wrong — coffee still in the cup, a record still on the turntable.

Over one long night, you'll move room to room, picking up the small things people leave behind: a half-written letter, a photograph turned face-down, a calendar with one date circled hard enough to tear the paper. Each clue you examine reshapes what you think you know. And every so often, the story stops and waits — because what happens next is up to you.

Apartment 520 is a narrative mystery in the spirit of a great short story: no fail states, no busywork, just you, a quiet room, and the slow pull of wanting to know the truth.

What's inside

A small story, told well

Examine everything

Pick up, turn over, and look closely at the objects in each room. The truth is hiding in the details.

Choices that matter

Key moments branch the story. Your decisions shape relationships, reveal new rooms, and lead to one of several endings.

A case you build

Every clue is filed in your case book. Connect them yourself — the game never solves the mystery for you.

Finish in one sitting

Around 90 minutes from key to ending. Respect for your time is baked in — pause and resume anywhere.

Original soundtrack

A hushed, hand-played score that breathes with the story. Best experienced with headphones, late at night.

Premium & complete

One purchase, the whole story. No ads, no in-app nickel-and-diming, no second-guessing whether you got the "real" ending.
