How to play

Easy to learn, lovely to master

Hold & drag a bubble

Press and hold any bubble, then drag it toward another one on the board.

Merge matching numbers

When two bubbles share the same number, they combine into a single bubble with a higher number.

Merge more, score more

Keep merging to grow bigger numbers. We track how many bubbles you merge and add up the score of every bubble in the combo.

Restart anytime

Tap the restart button to clear the board and start fresh. No timers, no game over — just relax and play endlessly.

Questions, answered

How much does Bubble Merge - Classic cost?

Bubble Merge - Classic is free to download and play.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole game runs on your device, so you can play on a plane or anywhere with no signal.

Which devices are supported?

Bubble Merge - Classic runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17.0 or later. It's tuned to work smoothly even on older devices.

Will my progress sync across devices?

Yes — if you're signed in to iCloud, your high scores sync automatically between your iPhone and iPad.
